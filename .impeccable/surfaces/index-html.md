# Surface brief: index.html (GJIC one-page site)

Scope: the whole public site, one page. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Slovenian policyholders (individuals, families, SMEs) who suspect they overpay or are under-covered and want an advisor who is not an insurer's agent. Action: request a free policy review via the contact section. Proof: the independent comparison across 13 partner insurers (shown as an authored, labelled demo comparison sheet), the real partner logos, and the founder himself. Constraints: single inlined HTML file, Slovenian, phone + desktop, logo scrolls away while nav stays fixed, no shield/protection clichés beyond the logo, no eyebrow labels, no scattered captions, no identical icon-card rows, contact form frontend-only, unknown facts are labelled demo placeholders.

Memorable moment: the comparison sheet. A real document on a navy desk showing one (fictional, labelled) household's policies, current premium vs. GJIC's recommendation across insurers, with tabular figures. The visitor leaves remembering "he showed me what the comparison actually looks like".

Unresolved: all figures, contact details, years of experience, fee model, licence text are demo and listed in the handoff for replacement.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the advisor's office with the client's documents laid out on the desk. Independence is demonstrated, not claimed: a real comparison sheet across insurers sits in the page as the central object. It refuses the category arrangement of stock-family hero, three icon cards, stat row and testimonial carousel.

OWN-WORLD: The materials visible in the founder's portrait, taken literally: plaster-white ground (#F3F1EC), navy wool (#15213A) that owns whole regions (the desk band holding the comparison sheet, the closing contact band), anodised-aluminium hairline rules (#A9ADB3), near-black ink (#121419), and the plant's green used only as the semantic "covered/better" mark (#2F6B4A). One typeface, Schibsted Grotesk, self-hosted: display 700 tight-tracked and large, body 400 at 18px, tabular numerals in every figure. Documents are drawn as sheets: white rectangles, 2px corners, 1px hairlines, one soft offset shadow. No pills, no gradients, no icons beyond an authored 1.5px-stroke mark set used sparingly inside controls.

STORY: The visitor understands GJIC compares insurers for them and is paid to be on their side; believes it because the comparison, the 13 real logos and the person are in front of them; and books a free review of their policies.
